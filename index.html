The Human Flourishing Program

Forgiveness means replacing ill will with goodwill toward those who have wronged us.

We have an evidence-based workbook, the REACH Forgiveness Workbook, that you can complete alone or in a group.

A Forgiveness Group allows you to work through the evidence-based REACH Forgiveness Workbook in a group setting that provides solidarity, accountability, and community. Do parts of the workbook on your own and then meet with your Forgiveness Group to discuss the experience of working through the workbook. Forgiveness Groups are easy to facilitate—online videos discuss the educational aspects of the workbook, take your group through an embodied experiential activity, and leave you with discussion questions to discuss with the group. All the tools you need are available on this site.

Start wherever you like. You can stop at any point and come back to it later.

The method

REACH

REACH is the method the workbook teaches. You work through it across twelve lessons, at whatever pace suits you.

REACH was developed by Everett Worthington and is the most widely tested forgiveness model.

    Forgiveness does not mean forgetting, excusing, or reconciling. It is compatible with accountability, truth, and justice.

    The evidence

    The REACH Forgiveness Workbook works.

    These results come from people working through the REACH Forgiveness Workbook on their own. A Forgiveness Group is a way to work through that same workbook alongside other people.
