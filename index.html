The method

REACH Forgiveness

REACH Forgiveness is the method the workbook teaches. You work through it across twelve lessons, at whatever pace suits you.

REACH Forgiveness was developed by Everett Worthington and is the most widely tested forgiveness model.

    Forgiveness does not mean forgetting, excusing, or reconciling. It is compatible with accountability, truth, and justice.
