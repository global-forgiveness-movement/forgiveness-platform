Forgiveness Groups

Doing this work alongside other people changes it

A Forgiveness Group is six to twelve people working through the REACH workbook on their own and meeting to talk about the experience. You don’t need to be a therapist or an expert to lead one — we provide the videos, the guidance, and the materials.

What happens in a Forgiveness Group

In a Forgiveness Group, participants work through the evidence-based REACH Forgiveness Workbook on their own and then meet periodically to discuss their experience with the material. The group provides solidarity, support, and accountability in a community setting.

Participants are encouraged to start with a small hurt as they learn about the REACH process and build their “forgiveness muscle.” No one is ever asked to share the specific hurt they are working through, which helps ensure the group remains a safe space to explore insights about forgiveness without divulging personal information.

Each meeting runs one to one and a half hours. The leader plays a video — 8 to 12 minutes of educational content and a guided embodied activity — and the group spends the rest of the session on the reflection questions it raises. There is a 3-session series to use with the REACH Forgiveness Workbook and a 6-session series to use with the REACH Workbook Adapted for Churches.

The REACH workbooks and Forgiveness Groups are not therapy and not a replacement for professional mental-health support. Start with a small hurt, not the largest thing you carry — like training before a marathon. If you ever need to skip a section or stop, you should: each person is on their own forgiveness journey, and not everyone will reach full emotional forgiveness. That’s okay. Forgiveness is a choice.

Running a group, step by step

Step 1

Choose your format

Three sessions using the standard workbook, or six sessions using the church edition.

Step 2

Gather 6–12 people

A church small group, a class, a workplace team, or friends.

Step 3

Everyone gets the workbook

Free PDF or print. Participants do the lessons on their own before each meeting.

Step 4

Press play, then talk

Each session: watch the short video together, then discuss. The video carries the structure so you don’t have to.
