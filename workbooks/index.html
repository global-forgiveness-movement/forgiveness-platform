The REACH Forgiveness Workbook

A self-guided workbook designed to take two to three hours. It was tested in a randomized trial with over 4,500 participants across 6 sites in 5 countries. You can download it, print it, share it, and translate it for your community. It is free, and it will stay free.

The interactive workbook · free account

The same twelve lessons, one gentle step at a time, with timers and a growing tree as you go. Make a free account and it remembers where you got to, on any device, so you can stop mid-lesson and come back.

What you write is never sent to us. Your answers are saved on your own device and stay there — account or no account. The only thing an account stores is how far you have got: which lesson you are on and which ones you have finished. Never a word of what you wrote.
