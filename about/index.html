About the movement

Using evidence-based interventions to make the world a more loving and forgiving place

The Global Forgiveness Movement is an initiative of the Human Flourishing Program. We take one of the most widely tested models of forgiveness in psychology, put it into free workbooks, and help people and communities everywhere actually work through it.

What we believe

A world where everyone is skilled at forgiving. Not a world without wrongdoing, and not a world where wrongs go unnamed — forgiveness is compatible with accountability, truth, and justice.

How we work

Rigorous research first, then free materials, then the people and communities who carry them. Everything we publish is grounded in evidence and given away.

Where we are going

Into every willing heart, home, and homeland. Materials already exist in the primary languages of more than two-thirds of the world’s population.

Partners & collaborators

The international trial was supported by the Templeton World Charity Foundation and carried out with partners in Colombia, Hong Kong, Indonesia, South Africa, and Ukraine, alongside Virginia Commonwealth University. The Salam Institute for Peace and Justice is leading an Arabic adaptation.

Part of the Human Flourishing Program

Since 2016, the Human Flourishing Program has studied what it means to live well and worked to translate that knowledge into efforts that strengthen people, communities, and institutions.
