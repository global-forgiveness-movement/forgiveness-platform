About the movement

Using evidence-based interventions to make the world a more loving and forgiving place

The Global Forgiveness Movement is an initiative of the Human Flourishing Program. We take one of the most widely tested models of forgiveness in psychology, put it into free workbooks, and help people and communities everywhere actually work through it.

What we believe

We believe in a world where everyone is skilled at forgiving. Not a world without wrongdoing, and not a world where wrongs go unnamed — forgiveness is compatible with accountability, truth, and justice.

How we work

We start with rigorous research, turn it into free materials, and put those materials in the hands of the people and communities who carry them. Everything we publish is grounded in evidence, and all of it is given away.

Where we are going

We are heading into every willing heart, home, and homeland. Our materials already exist in the primary languages of more than two-thirds of the world’s population.

The people behind this

How this work is funded

The international REACH forgiveness trial was funded by the Templeton World Charity Foundation. The Global Forgiveness Movement is funded by Bancel Philanthropies.

Who is behind this site

This site is published by the Global Forgiveness Movement, an initiative within the Human Flourishing Program at Harvard University. Since 2016, the program has studied what it means to live well and worked to translate that knowledge into efforts that strengthen people, communities, and institutions.
