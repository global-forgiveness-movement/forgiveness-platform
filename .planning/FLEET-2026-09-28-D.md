## Why — the bug Wyatt hit, live

On the live site Wyatt ticked "Everyone has their workbook" on his group `GFM-3-4NXT` and got
"That didn't save." Cause, measured by the coordinator:
- `firestore.rules` decides who may write a group's state from `groupCodes/{code}.facilitator`.
- The page decides "you are the facilitator" from `members/{uid}.facilitator` (the flag `roleFor`
  in `js/group-engine.js` reads).
- All three live groups (`GFM-3-4NXT` Sept 24, `GFM-3-XWPP` and `GFM-6-GPQQ` Sept 27) were created
  before the engine, so their `groupCodes` record names no facilitator. The page offers ticks the
  database will always refuse. Two of them may belong to Kate or Richard.
**Two places decide "who facilitates this group". After this lane: one.**

## Wyatt's rulings, 2026-09-28 — never re-ask

1. **A person can be in several groups at once** — lead one or more, and follow others.
2. **No scary warning.** "Remove this group from My Path" (a `confirm()` telling people to write the
   code down) goes. In its place: **"Finished with this group"** moves the group to a **Past groups**
   section at the bottom of My Path, code still visible, with **"Bring back"** one tap away. Because
   nothing is lost, there is no confirmation dialog.
3. **Build it now** — it ships before the call on September 29.

## What to build

1. **ONE fact: who facilitates a group is `groupCodes/{code}.facilitator`.** The engine's role comes
   from that and nothing else; delete the `facilitator` flag from memberships as a decider. Extend
   `scripts/qa/group-engine-onehelm.mjs` (or add a check) so it goes red if the page decides
   facilitation from anything but the group code's record.
2. **The three older groups get their facilitator back.** Each registration in `groups/` (editor-read
   only) carries the `userId` that created it. Add an editor-only action in `/admin` — one button, one
   line explaining it — that, for every `groupCodes` doc with no `facilitator`, finds its registration
   and writes that `userId` as `facilitator` (editors may update `groupCodes` under the current rules).
   It reports what it did. Never guess: a code with no matching registration is listed, not assigned.
3. **Many groups.** Memberships live alongside the person's name in `members/{uid}` (that doc also holds
   `name` and `newsletter` — do not clobber them), as a map keyed by group code:
   `groups: { "GFM-3-4NXT": { seriesId, status: "active" | "past", joinedAt, pastAt? } }`. Keep reading
   the old single-group shape (`code`, `seriesId`, `facilitator`) and convert it on first read, so no
   one loses their group. Member lesson ticks move to per-group too (`memberTicks/{uid}` keyed by code),
   still owner-only.
4. **My Path.**
   - **Your groups:** one card per active group, lead or follow, showing series, code, and the next
     meeting. Choosing a card shows that group's series below (the existing engine + display, for that
     one group). With exactly one active group, it is simply shown.
   - **Start another group** and **Join a group with a code** are always available (not only when in
     no group) — the same `js/group-create.js` and join flow, never forked.
   - **Past groups:** at the bottom, collapsed by default when empty-ish; each shows series and code,
     and **Bring back**. "Finished with this group" is reversible, so no dialog.
5. **firestore.rules.** `inGroup(code)` must read the new map (and keep accepting the old shape until
   converted). `memberTicks` rules follow the new shape. Owner-only and facilitator-only lines stay as
   strict as they are. Write Wyatt's paste instruction.
6. **Also, one line:** on the Groups page a signed-in person still sees "Create your facilitator
   account". Signed in, it should read "Create a group" and go to the same place.
7. **Account deletion** still erases everything the account stores: every membership, every tick, and
   the group state of every group it facilitates.
