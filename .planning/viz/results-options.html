Three simpler ways to show the drop

Each picture shows one thing: in two weeks, people who did the workbook felt about a third less depression, anxiety and ill will. People who hadn’t started yet barely changed.

Real data: each group’s average score before and two weeks later (Ho et al., BMJ Public Health 2024, Supplementary Table S7). “How much it fell” is measured from each scale’s floor, where zero means none at all.
