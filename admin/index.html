How this editor works

Every word on the site is edited on the page itself — headings, paragraphs, buttons, and the words in the lists too: testimonials, people, publications, events, workbook cards. Open any page, press Edit page text in the bottom-right corner, click the outlined words and type. Save puts it live.

This page is for everything else: adding, removing and reordering the items in a list, setting video links, building new pages, reading what people send through the site, and bringing back an earlier version.

Every save — here or on a page — is kept under History. Restoring an old version keeps the one it replaces, so nothing is ever lost.
